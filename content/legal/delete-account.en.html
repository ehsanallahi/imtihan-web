Delete your Imtihan account

In the app

  1. Open Imtihan and go to the Me tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email support.imtihan@gmail.com from, or mentioning, the phone number or Google account you signed in with. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in, profile, quiz attempts, topic progress, study plan, usage counters, notification tokens, waiting-list entry and scanned photos are permanently deleted. Crash reports and usage statistics, which are not linked to your name, expire on Firebase's own schedule.

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026 · Imtihan is a product of Ravi Stack

Imtihan is an exam-preparation app for Matric (Class 9 and 10) students in Pakistan. This policy explains in plain language what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who can use Imtihan

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. If you are under 18, please read this policy with a parent or guardian. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your phone number if you sign in by phone, or your Google account name and ID if you sign in with Google. Guests get an anonymous ID only.
  • Profile you give us: class (9th or 10th), study group, board, and preferred study language. If you choose to add them: your name, city, school or academy, exam date, and a parent's WhatsApp number.
  • Learning activity: the questions you answer, your scores, time taken, topic mastery, and study streak.
  • AI tutor: the questions you type and the textbook questions you scan. Scanned photos are stored privately for up to 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Notifications: a device token, so we can send the reminders and alerts you turn on, including when questions for your board and class are ready.
  • Plan: your plan, when it ends, and any activation code you redeem.
  • App health: crash reports and basic anonymous usage statistics to help us fix bugs and keep the app fast.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or your advertising ID.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep your progress synced across your devices.
  • To build your personalized daily study plan and spaced repetition schedule.
  • To answer your academic doubts with the AI tutor.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Who else handles your data

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud store your account and activity securely and run our servers.
  • Google Gemini receives the academic questions and textbook scans you send to the AI tutor so that it can answer them. We do not send your name or phone number with them.

These services may process data outside Pakistan.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and learning activity until you delete your account. Scanned textbook photos are deleted after 30 days. Crash reports and usage statistics are kept for the periods set by Firebase.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time in the Me tab.
  • Delete your account and all its data from Me → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for a correction, at the contact below.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption. Each student's records can be read only by that student's own account.

Contact

Privacy questions, data requests and support: support.imtihan@gmail.com

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
